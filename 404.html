What the columns mean
RTB pos rank by draft $ — where his price ranked at his position among players bought at that year’s draft (Drafted or Rookie); RB4 means three drafted RBs cost more. Keepers, franchise tags, devy and taxi players show —.
Fantasy Pros ADP — Fantasy Pros average draft position across all positions around draft time.
Fantasy Pros pos ADP — the same, within his position; WR1 is the top WR off boards.
Points — total fantasy points he scored in the season he was drafted.
EOS rank — end-of-season finish across all positions that year.
EOS pos rank — end-of-season finish at his position; WR5 = the #5 scoring WR.
